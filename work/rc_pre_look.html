🌲 WOODS DRONE SIM

Endless woods, hoops, slalom gates, a twisty forest trail and a race arena with light tunnels. Build your own drone, race your ghost, pop balloons, fix crashes, fly with friends. Works with your DJI remote (USB or the Phone Link), a controller, keyboard or touch — and in VR goggles.

Start like a real DJI: pull BOTH sticks down and in (or down and out) → motors start → push the left stick up. · Right stick = fly · wheel = camera tilt (hold Fn + wheel = zoom) · PHOTO = photo / record / fire · PHOTO-VIDEO switch = photo → video → paintball · Fn tap = next camera · Fn hold = menu · ⏮ tap = fly home, double-tap = land · C/N/S = speed
